# 10x Designers — Design Community & Inspiration

> **A community and gallery of exceptional web design.** Built with Framer. Features portfolio, branding, and animated website examples.

**Homepage:** https://10xdesigners.co
**Framer Gallery:** https://www.framer.com/gallery/10xdesigners
**Pricing:** Free (community)
**Platform:** Framer (the site itself is built on Framer)

---

## Overview

10x Designers is an online community offering live design sessions, workshops, and design content. Its website serves as a **showcase gallery** — particularly relevant for portfolio and personal brand design inspiration.

On Framer's gallery, 10x Designers curates collections of exceptional Framer-built websites, including:
- Personal portfolios
- Dark mode designs
- Animated websites
- Color-driven design
- Branding showcases

## Key Strengths

- **Free resource** — no subscription needed
- **Portfolio-focused** — directly relevant for personal site inspiration
- **Framer ecosystem** — if you use Framer, directly applicable
- **Dual purpose** — community + gallery

---

## Key Weaknesses

- **Framer-focused** — most showcased sites are built with Framer, not code
- **Smaller library** — not as comprehensive as [[Godly]] or Awwwards
- **No structure** — visual browsing only
- **No AI integration**
