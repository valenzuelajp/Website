# Cinematic Website Build Plan

This plan breaks the portfolio build into clear steps so the website evolves from a good landing page into a true cinematic experience.

## Step 1 — Review the current experience
- Check the site as a visitor would.
- Identify what feels polished, what feels generic, and what feels unfinished.
- Keep notes in [[Progress-Tracker]] and [[Changelog]].

## Step 2 — Strengthen the first impression
- Improve the hero copy so it feels more intentional and personal.
- Make the opening section feel like a story, not just an introduction.
- Add stronger motion and better contrast between headline, subtext, and CTA.

## Step 3 — Create a clearer narrative
- Make the site feel like a journey: intro → story → work → proof → contact.
- Use section transitions, spacing, and motion to guide the visitor.
- Ensure each section answers a different question: who you are, what you do, what you built, why you matter.

## Step 4 — Elevate the projects section
- Turn projects into a premium showcase with stronger imagery, outcomes, and narrative.
- Highlight the problem, the solution, and the impact.
- Keep the design aligned with the cinematic dark theme.

## Step 5 — Polish the details
- Improve typography, spacing, and rhythm.
- Add stronger hover states, section reveals, and micro-interactions.
- Keep accessibility and mobile usability in mind.

## Step 6 — Final polish and deployment
- Review performance and loading.
- Add final SEO and metadata improvements.
- Prepare deployment and document the last changes in [[Changelog]].

## Immediate Next Task
- Refine the hero and section storytelling before adding more visual effects.
